# Changelog

## Unreleased
### New
- Keyboard shortcuts on desktop and the web, listed in Settings: switch and close tabs, change the layout, step to the next sutta, resize text and more

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-08
### New
- Open on SuttaCentral in the Share menu, naming whose English translation it opens

### Improved
- Recent searches show five, with Show more for the rest
- Search opens references like SN 15:3, Tha Ap 1 and Dhp 5

### Fixed
- Sinhala shows its own printed page number, not the Pali one
- The stacked layout shows a sutta number once, not twice
- Research citations like AN 1.1 open their text, and each source shows once a paragraph

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-01
### New
- Singlish: R types repaya, and c types ච
- Definitions in search results open in full with Show more

### Improved
- Search finds words anywhere in a sutta, not just in one paragraph
- Clearer icons, steadier find in page, a 50-tab limit

### Fixed
- On the web, the app opens with no connection after one online visit
- Refine finds text in every section you tick
- Search works with sutta numbers like 3.1.4.1, and with : or ?
- Commentary results show their text instead of an error

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-25
The first release: a web app and a static site

### New
- app.sammaditthi.net: the full app, where new features land
- The Buddha Jayanti Tipiṭaka in Pali and Sinhala, side by side or stacked, with reader settings to suit you
- Search the whole canon, and look up Pali words in Sinhala and English dictionaries
- Research: ask a question and get an answer cited to SuttaCentral's translations
- sammaditthi.net: a static site of light pages for search engines, AI training and slow connections
